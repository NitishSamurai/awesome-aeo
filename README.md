## What is AEO?

**Answer Engine Optimization (AEO)** is the practice of optimizing your content, entity presence, and technical signals so that AI answer engines surface and cite your brand when users ask questions in your space.

It overlaps with several related terms:

- **GEO** (Generative Engine Optimization) — optimizing for generative answers specifically.
- **LLMO** (LLM Optimization) — optimizing for large language models.
- **AI SEO** — the broader umbrella of SEO adapted for AI search.

In practice these are used interchangeably. The goal is the same: be the source the AI names.
